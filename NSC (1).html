2. Risk Assessment

Low Risk

  • > 32 week gestation
  • > 1500 g birth weight
  • No other risk factors

Moderate Risk

  • 28+1 - 31+6 gestation or VLBW 1000-1500g
  • Complex congenital cardiac disease
  • Dexamethasone treatment
  • Indomethacin or Ibuprofen treatment for PDA
  • Inotropic support
  • Symptomatic polycythaemia
  • Previous NEC

High Risk

  • < 28 weeks gestation or ELBW < 1000g
  • Absent or reversed end diastolic flow in infants < 34 weeks
  • Perinatal hypoxia-ischaemia with organ dysfunction
  • Hypotensive / unstable ventilated neonates
  • Commencing feeds after an episode of NEC or GI surgery
3. Initiations and Advancement

Low Risk Path

Step 1

Introduce buccal colostrum as soon as possible after birth.

Step 2

Start full enteral feeds 60-90 mL/kg/day as 2 to 3 hourly boluses.

Step 3

Advance feeds as tolerated 30-40 mL/kg/day.

Moderate - High Risk Path

First 24 Hours

Start by colostrum - start TPN.

24 - 48 Hours

Continue colostrum until enough MBM available for trophic feeds (10-20 ml/kg/day).

Advancement

20-30 ml/kg/day. Decrease PN only once on total fluids of 150ml/kg/day.
